## 1. Why this matters

Paying micro-entity fees ($70 + $154 + $176 = **$400** at filing per the USPTO fee schedule read 2026-10-25 — see 00 README §4) requires that the certification be **true for every applicant, every inventor, and every party holding or promised an ownership interest**. An erroneous assertion made in good faith can usually be corrected by paying the fee deficiency (37 CFR 1.29(k)), but a knowingly false certification is fraud on the Office. When in doubt, pay the **small-entity** rate instead ($70 + $308 + $352 = $730; verify at filing).

## 2. The four requirements (37 CFR 1.29(a)) — as stated on the USPTO micro entity page

Source: https://www.uspto.gov/PatentMicroentity (page last updated Sep 17, 2026; read 2026-10-25). To qualify on the **gross income basis**, **all four** must be true:

| # | Requirement (paraphrased from 37 CFR 1.29(a)(1)–(4)) | COE Security LLC — status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Small entity.** The applicant, each inventor, and any other party with an ownership interest qualifies as a small entity under 37 CFR 1.27 (e.g., a business concern with ≤ 500 employees including affiliates, and no rights assigned/licensed/obligated to a non-small entity). | [APPLICANT TO CONFIRM] — COE Security LLC appears to be a small business; confirm employee count including affiliates and that no license/obligation to a large entity exists. |
| 2 | **Application filing limit.** Neither the applicant nor any inventor has been **named as an inventor on more than four previously filed U.S. patent applications**, excluding provisionals, foreign applications, PCT applications for which the basic national fee was not paid, and applications resulting from the inventor's prior employment where rights were assigned to a previous employer. | [APPLICANT TO CONFIRM for EACH inventor] — count every prior nonprovisional U.S. application on which the person was named. |
| 3 | **Income limit — applicants and inventors.** In the **calendar year preceding** the year in which the fee is paid, neither the applicant nor any inventor had a **gross income** exceeding the **maximum qualifying gross income** (three times the median household income as reported by the Census Bureau). For "gross income" the USPTO looks to the IRS definition (total income, **not** adjusted gross income). | [APPLICANT TO CONFIRM for COE Security LLC (as applicant) AND for each inventor] — for a fee paid in 2026 the relevant year is **2025**. |
| 4 | **Income limit — parties with an ownership interest.** Neither the applicant nor any inventor has **assigned, granted, or conveyed, or is under an obligation to assign, grant or convey**, a license or other ownership interest to an entity that exceeded the income limit in the preceding calendar year. | [APPLICANT TO CONFIRM] — the inventor(s) assign to COE Security LLC; therefore COE Security LLC's own 2025 gross income must be at or below the limit. Confirm no other licensee. |

- **$262,380** — stated on the USPTO micro entity page as applying from **September 15, 2026 to present** (page banner: "As of September 15, 2026"), read 2026-10-25. Source: https://www.uspto.gov/PatentMicroentity

If COE Security LLC's 2025 gross income (total revenue as reported for tax purposes — confirm the IRS definition with the practitioner/accountant) exceeded $262,380, **micro-entity status is not available** and small-entity fees apply.

### Who may sign (VERIFY)

Under 37 CFR 1.29(e) and 1.33(b), the micro-entity certification may be signed by a registered practitioner of record, or by an applicant (for a juristic-entity applicant, an officer — e.g., a member/manager of the LLC — is generally permitted to sign the **entity-status assertion** even though a practitioner must handle prosecution). **VERIFY** with the practitioner which signer is appropriate; if a practitioner is retained, have the practitioner sign.

## 4. Continuing obligations

- Micro-entity status must be **re-certified at each fee payment** in the sense that the status must still be true when each fee is paid (issue fee, maintenance fees). Notify the USPTO of loss of entitlement before paying a fee at the micro rate (37 CFR 1.29(g)–(i)).
- If any inventor is named on a fifth nonprovisional application, or any party's gross income exceeds the limit, status is lost.
