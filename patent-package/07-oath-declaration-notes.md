## 1. Oath or declaration (35 U.S.C. 115; 37 CFR 1.63)

**Which form:** because this application uses an Application Data Sheet (ADS, see 05) that identifies the inventors and the applicant, use **form PTO/AIA/01 — "Declaration (37 CFR 1.63) For Utility Or Design Application Using An Application Data Sheet (37 CFR 1.76)"** (USPTO forms list shows revision 06/2012). PDF: https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aia0001.pdf (**VERIFY** current revision on https://www.uspto.gov/patents/apply/forms). The alternative form **PTO/AIA/08** (revision 11/2015, https://www.uspto.gov/sites/default/files/documents/aia0008.pdf) is for applications **not** using an ADS — not needed here.

**What each inventor declares (in substance — read the form):**
- the application was made or authorized to be made by the declarant;
- the declarant believes they are the original inventor or an original joint inventor of a claimed invention;
- acknowledgment that willful false statements are punishable by fine or imprisonment (18 U.S.C. 1001) and may jeopardize the validity of the application or any resulting patent.

**Rules to observe:**
- One AIA/01 per inventor (each inventor signs their own), **or** a single declaration listing all inventors if the form version allows — the common practice is one per inventor.
- The inventor's name on the declaration must **match the ADS exactly**.
- The declaration must identify the application it relates to; when signed at filing, the title must match and the declaration should be executed **after** the inventor has reviewed the specification and claims as filed (the inventor must have reviewed and understood the contents — 37 CFR 1.63(c)).
- **Timing:** the declaration may be filed with the application or **postponed** until no later than the date the issue fee is paid, **provided** an ADS naming the inventors is filed at filing (37 CFR 1.53(f)(3)). A surcharge applies if it is not present at filing: fee code 1051/2051/3051, "Surcharge - Late filing fee, search fee, examination fee, inventor's oath or declaration" (37 CFR 1.16(f)) — **$170 undiscounted / $68 small / $34 micro** as read from the USPTO fee schedule on 2026-10-25 (**verify at filing**). Recommended: file it **with** the application to avoid the surcharge.
- **Substitute statement (PTO/AIA/02):** if an inventor is deceased, legally incapacitated, cannot be found after diligent effort, or **refuses to sign** despite an obligation to assign, the applicant (COE Security LLC) may file a substitute statement in lieu of the declaration. Not expected to be needed; noted for completeness.

**Signature method:** S-signature (e.g., `/Jane Q. Inventor/`) typed between forward slashes is acceptable on USPTO forms (37 CFR 1.4(d)(2)), or a handwritten signature scanned to PDF. Patent Center also supports its own e-signature flow for some forms — follow the on-screen instructions.

## 3. Inventorship determination (do this before signing anything)

- Inventorship is **claim-by-claim**: an inventor is a person who contributed to the **conception** of at least one claim as filed. Persons who only implemented another's conception, tested it, or managed the project are not inventors; persons who conceived any claimed feature (e.g., the on-device OCR line-mask approach, the token map design, the gating rule) are.
- Review the claim set in 04 with every candidate and record who conceived what (a short dated memo is good practice).
- Naming the wrong inventors can be corrected (37 CFR 1.48) but deliberate misnaming invalidates the patent.
- **Note on AI-assisted drafting:** this package was drafted with the help of an AI tool. Under current USPTO guidance (**VERIFY** the then-current "Inventorship Guidance for AI-Assisted Inventions," Federal Register Feb. 13, 2024, and any superseding guidance), only natural persons who made a significant contribution to conception may be named; the drafting assistance for this application does not itself create inventorship. The inventive concepts should be those of the named human inventor(s) as implemented in the WorkCoPilot product before this package was prepared.
