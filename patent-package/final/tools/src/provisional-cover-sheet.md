# Provisional application cover sheet (37 CFR 1.51(c)(1)): content of form PTO/SB/16

## Which form

A provisional application needs a cover sheet that identifies it as a provisional application, names the inventor, gives the title and gives a correspondence address (37 CFR 1.51(c)(1)). The USPTO form is PTO/SB/16, "Provisional Application for Patent Cover Sheet". Download the current revision from the USPTO forms page, complete it in Adobe Reader from the entries below, save it with the form fields intact, and upload it in Patent Center under the document description "Provisional Cover Sheet (SB16)". Patent Center also offers a web ADS for provisional applications; either is acceptable, and the entries are the same. Do not file both.

Every entry below is filled from the applicant intake. The only line completed on the day of filing is the signature line.
