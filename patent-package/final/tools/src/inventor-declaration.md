# Inventor's declaration (35 U.S.C. 115, 37 CFR 1.63): content of form PTO/AIA/01

## Which form

The declaration is executed on the current USPTO form PTO/AIA/01, "Declaration (37 CFR 1.63) for Utility or Design Application Using an Application Data Sheet (37 CFR 1.76)". This form is used because an Application Data Sheet (application-data-sheet.md) is filed with the application and supplies the inventor's residence and mailing address, so the declaration itself does not repeat them. Download the current revision from the USPTO forms page, complete it in Adobe Reader, sign it as described in section 4, save it and upload it in Patent Center under the document description "Oath or Declaration filed" (document code OATH).

The content below is the full content of the form, filled in. Read and sign the official form; this page is the record of what goes on it.

## 2. Statutory statements printed on the form

By signing, the inventor makes the following statements, which are the statements required by 35 U.S.C. 115 and 37 CFR 1.63. They are reproduced here so that the inventor can read them before signing; the signed text is the text printed on the official form.

As the below named inventor, I hereby declare that:

1. The above-identified application was made or authorized to be made by me.
2. I believe that I am the original inventor or an original joint inventor of a claimed invention in the application.
3. I hereby acknowledge that any willful false statement made in this declaration is punishable under 18 U.S.C. 1001 by fine or imprisonment of not more than five (5) years, or both.

The form also prints a warning that petitioners and applicants are cautioned to avoid submitting personal information in documents filed in a patent application that may contribute to identity theft (for example social security numbers, bank account numbers or credit card numbers). No such information appears anywhere in this package.

## 4. How to sign

Either of the following is acceptable (37 CFR 1.4(d)):

1. S-signature. In the signature field, the inventor personally types his name between forward slashes: /Ravi Kiran Raju Yerra/. The person whose name appears must be the one who types it. Save the PDF; no printing is needed.
2. Handwritten signature. Print the completed form, sign in ink, and scan to PDF at 300 dpi or better, black and white, US Letter.

Do not sign on behalf of the inventor; no one else may sign this declaration. The signature date is the date the signature is applied. The declaration is valid regardless of how long before filing it was signed, provided the application filed is the one the inventor reviewed.

## 5. Timing and surcharge

File the signed declaration in the same Patent Center submission as the application. If it is omitted, the application still receives a filing date because the ADS names the inventor (37 CFR 1.53(f)(3)), but a surcharge under 37 CFR 1.16(f) becomes due and the declaration must be filed no later than the payment of the issue fee. Filing it with the application avoids the surcharge and the follow-up notice.

## 6. Substitute statement

A substitute statement on PTO/AIA/02 is used only when an inventor is deceased, legally incapacitated, cannot be found or refuses to sign. None of these applies; no substitute statement is prepared.
