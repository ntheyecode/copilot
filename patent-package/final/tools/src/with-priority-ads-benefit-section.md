Rules that govern this section:

1. Deadline. This nonprovisional application must be filed within twelve months of the provisional filing date (35 U.S.C. 119(e)(1)). The twelve-month period runs to the same calendar day one year later; if that day falls on a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday in the District of Columbia, the period runs to the next business day (35 U.S.C. 21(b)). Do not rely on that extension; file earlier.
2. Timing of the benefit claim. The claim is made in the ADS, and it must be presented within the later of four months from the actual filing date of this application or sixteen months from the filing date of the provisional application (37 CFR 1.78(a)(4)). Entering it in the ADS at filing, as this package does, satisfies the rule.
3. Late claim. A benefit claim presented after that period requires a petition under 37 CFR 1.78(c) with the petition fee and a statement that the delay was unintentional. Avoid this by entering the two values at filing.
4. Restoration. If the twelve-month period is missed by no more than two months, a petition under 37 CFR 1.78(b) to restore the benefit is possible, with a petition fee and a statement of unintentional delay. Treat this as an emergency route only.
5. The provisional and this application must name at least one inventor in common. Both name Ravi Kiran Raju Yerra as the sole inventor.
