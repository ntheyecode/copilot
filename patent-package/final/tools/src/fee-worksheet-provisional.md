# Fee worksheet: provisional application, small entity

Entity status: small entity (37 CFR 1.27), asserted on the provisional cover sheet (PTO/SB/16) and by paying the small entity rate. Micro entity status is not claimed because the applicant's gross income exceeds the micro entity limit.

The amounts are the small entity amounts of the USPTO fee schedule as read on 2026-10-25 and recorded in this package. Every line is marked "verify current USPTO fee schedule at filing"; Patent Center computes the total at submission.

## 2. Fees that do not apply to a provisional application

| Fee | Why it does not apply |
|---|---|
| Search fee and examination fee (1.16(k), 1.16(o)) | A provisional application is never searched or examined. |
| Excess-claim fees (1.16(h), (i), (j)) | Claims are not required in a provisional application and are not counted for fees even when included, as they are here. |
| Non-DOCX surcharge (1.16(u)) | The surcharge applies only to nonprovisional applications under 35 U.S.C. 111(a). The provisional specification may be uploaded as DOCX or PDF without surcharge. |
| Oath or declaration surcharge (1.16(f)) | No declaration is filed with a provisional application. |
| Information Disclosure Statement fee (1.17(p)) | No IDS is filed in a provisional application. |

## 5. What the provisional fee does not buy

The provisional fee secures a filing date for twelve months. It does not count toward the nonprovisional fees: when the nonprovisional application claiming this provisional's benefit is filed within twelve months (patent-package/final/utility-with-priority), the basic filing, search, examination and excess-claim fees of that application are due in full at that time (see that package's fee-worksheet.md, 970 USD small entity as recorded for 20 claims with 4 independent claims, of which 240 USD is the one excess-independent-claim fee, or 730 USD if the fourth independent claim is also dropped; verify current USPTO fee schedule at filing).

## 6. How to pay

Patent Center accepts credit or debit card, electronic funds transfer from a bank account, and USPTO deposit account. Pay on the final screen of the submission and keep the Electronic Acknowledgement Receipt.
