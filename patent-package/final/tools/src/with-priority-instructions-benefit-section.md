This package already claims the benefit of the provisional application. Complete the benefit claim as follows; these are the only two values entered by hand anywhere in this package.

1. Open the provisional application's filing receipt (the Electronic Acknowledgement Receipt from the day of submission, or the official Filing Receipt in Patent Center). Copy the application number (format 63/xxx,xxx) and the filing date (the date the provisional was filed, not the date the receipt was printed).
2. Specification. In editable/application-body.docx (and, to keep the masters consistent, in specification.md, application-body.md and editable/specification.docx) replace "[ENTER PROVISIONAL APPLICATION NUMBER FROM FILING RECEIPT]" with the number and "[ENTER PROVISIONAL FILING DATE]" with the date written out (for example "January 15, 2027"), so that the first paragraph reads: "This application claims the benefit of U.S. Provisional Application No. 63/xxx,xxx, filed Month D, YYYY." Search the document for "[ENTER" afterwards to confirm no marker remains.
3. ADS. In the web ADS section "Domestic Benefit/National Stage Information" enter: Prior application status "Pending"; Application number (this application) left blank; Continuity type "Claims benefit of provisional application" (35 U.S.C. 119(e)); Prior application number: the provisional number; Filing date: the provisional filing date in YYYY-MM-DD form. application-data-sheet.md section 7 carries the same two markers.
4. Deadlines, both hard:
   The nonprovisional application must be filed no later than twelve months after the provisional filing date (35 U.S.C. 119(e)(1)). If the last day is a Saturday, Sunday or federal holiday the period runs to the next business day, but do not plan on it. A provisional cannot be extended; if the twelve months are missed by up to two months, only a petition under 37 CFR 1.78(b) with a fee and a statement of unintentional delay can restore the benefit.
   The benefit claim must be presented in the ADS within the later of four months from the filing date of this application or sixteen months from the provisional filing date (37 CFR 1.78(a)(4)). Entering it in the ADS at filing satisfies this; a claim added later needs a petition under 37 CFR 1.78(c) with a fee.
5. The provisional and this application both name Ravi Kiran Raju Yerra as sole inventor, which satisfies the common-inventor requirement of 35 U.S.C. 119(e).
6. After filing, check the Filing Receipt: it must list the provisional application under "Domestic Priority data as claimed by applicant" with the correct number and date. If it does not, file a corrected ADS within the period in item 4 and request a corrected Filing Receipt.
7. The provisional application itself requires nothing further. It is never examined and goes abandoned automatically at twelve months; its contents stay confidential unless this application, which refers to it, is published, after which the provisional's file becomes available to the public.
