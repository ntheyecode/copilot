# Filing instructions: provisional application (35 U.S.C. 111(b))

This package files a provisional application. A provisional application establishes a filing date for everything it discloses, costs little, is never examined and never published on its own, and expires twelve months after filing. Within those twelve months the nonprovisional application in patent-package/final/utility-with-priority is filed and claims this provisional's filing date for every claim the provisional supports. The provisional's specification is the same text as the nonprovisional's specification, with claims 1 to 29 and the abstract included, so the support is complete.

3. Note the earliest public disclosure, public use or offer for sale (the product is live at admireveda.com). ## 6. After submission

1. The official Filing Receipt arrives in Patent Center and by mail some weeks later. Check inventor name, title, filing date and entity status against the receipt; request correction if the USPTO made an error.
2. The Filing Receipt also states whether a foreign filing license under 35 U.S.C. 184 is granted; do not file abroad before the license or six months from filing.
3. Nothing else happens. The USPTO does not examine, search or publish a provisional application and sends no Office action.

## 7. The twelve months

| Date | What |
|---|---|
| Provisional filing date | Application number 63/xxx,xxx and filing date issued |
| Provisional filing date plus 12 months | Last day to file the nonprovisional application claiming this provisional's benefit (patent-package/final/utility-with-priority), and the last day for foreign or PCT applications claiming its priority. Both are hard deadlines. File the nonprovisional application well before, since the full nonprovisional fees (2,170 USD small entity as recorded for the 29-claim set, or 730 USD if trimmed to 20 claims with 3 independent claims; verify at filing) and the signed declaration are due then. |
| Provisional filing date plus 12 months and 2 months | Outer limit of a petition under 37 CFR 1.78(b) to restore the benefit if the twelve months were missed unintentionally; a fee and a statement are required. An emergency route only. |

The provisional application cannot be extended, renewed or converted into a second provisional. If no nonprovisional application is filed within twelve months, the provisional lapses and the filing date is lost. Filing a new provisional afterwards starts a new, later date and does not revive the old one.

## 8. Mistakes to avoid

1. Leaving the review line in the page header of the filed specification.
2. Selecting "Nonprovisional" by mistake; the fee screen would show search and examination fees and the application would be examined as filed.
3. Selecting micro entity, which is not available to this applicant.
4. Treating the provisional as the end of the process. Calendar the twelve-month date on the day of filing and plan the nonprovisional filing for well before it.
5. Losing the receipt. The application number and filing date on it are the two values entered into the utility-with-priority package.
